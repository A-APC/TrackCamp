The Experience

What to Expect

Every day runs the same proven structure — a dynamic warmup, three rotating event stations, and a group game to close it out. All energy.

1
Warmup

Every session kicks off with a group jog and dynamic drills led by Coach Foster to get athletes activated, focused, and competition-ready.

2
3 Rotating Event Stations

Athletes rotate through 3 different event stations — each one a different discipline. Small groups of up to 10 keep coaching focused and reps high. Every station, every day, is a different event so athletes experience the full range of track and field.

3
Group Game

Every day ends with a competitive group game that gets everyone involved — The perfect way to close out a hard day of training with team energy and a lot of fun.
