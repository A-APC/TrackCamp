About Me

Caleb Foster

With over 14 years of experience in track and field — 4 years at the University of Florida and 1 at the University of Oregon — I've competed at every level from collegiate to national and post-collegiate. A 2× D1 All-American, Big 10 Team Champion, and 3× University of Florida Team D1 National Champion, I bring elite experience directly to every athlete I coach.

My coaching philosophy is holistic and intentional — focused not just on physical mechanics and strength, but mental resilience, nutrition, and the personalized habits that lead to lasting success. All it takes is one practice, one cue, one jump to change everything.
